## 🧩 The Problem

Every day, founders spend countless hours tracking, joining, and replying to conversations across online communities like Hacker News, Reddit, and Product Hunt just to stay visible and relevant.
These interactions are powerful but time-consuming, manual, and often reactive.

<img width="3214" height="1754" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/08215053-768c-487c-84cf-a39bab29e40b" />

## ⚡ The Solution

WhatPeopleWant is an AI-powered agent that automatically:
- 🔍 **Discovers** relevant discussions about your product across online forums (starting with Hacker News)
- 💬 **Engages** in conversations that matter, saving you time and helping you connect with your audience
- 📊 **Analyzes** community sentiment and identifies opportunities for your product
- ⚡ **Notifies** you of high-impact discussions so you never miss important conversations
